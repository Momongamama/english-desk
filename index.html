      Dictionary and exam words

      Tap any word while reading the news to look it up. The site has the open-source ECDICT built in (Chinese and English definitions) plus your notes, so it works without importing anything. For full definitions and examples, import your own dictionary (e.g. english-desk-dict.ndjson.gz) and it will be checked first. Exam words work the same way: the built-in list is used until you import your CSV list. Imported files stay in this browser and are never uploaded, so import them once on your Mac and once on your iPhone.

      Underlines: solid for TOEFL, dashed for IELTS, wavy for GRE. On the Read page you can choose which ones to show, or turn them all off.

      Sync across devices

      Keep your cards, practice log and AI choices the same on your Mac and iPhone. They're saved in a secret gist on your GitHub account. Make a token that can only use gists, paste it here, then do the same on your other device.

      Make a token

      On the GitHub page, tick only "gist" and pick an expiration (or "No expiration"). API keys and your dictionary are never uploaded. A secret gist isn't listed anywhere, but anyone with its link can read it.

      Opening the site pulls the latest from GitHub, and changes are sent a few seconds after you save. API keys and your dictionary stay on each device.

      Backup

      Your cards and sessions are stored in this browser. Export a backup before you switch devices or browsers or clear site data, then import it in the new place. Importing merges the two: new cards are added, the newer review progress wins, and cards you deleted stay deleted.
